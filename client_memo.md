# StreamVault Film Licensing Recommendation

**To:** Kwame Boateng, VP Content Acquisition  
**Subject:** Recommendation for the $14M Film Licensing Review

## Recommendation

Give franchise deals priority during negotiations, but also consider a small number of individual films.

The 112 films we successfully reviewed show that films belonging to a franchise performed better as a group than films with no recorded franchise membership.

| Comparison | All years | 2000 onward |
|---|---:|---:|
| Franchise films | 96 films, 5.58x | 63 films, 5.08x |
| Films with no recorded franchise | 16 films, 4.85x | 8 films, 4.24x |

The results support focusing on franchises in both periods. However, these figures show cinema earnings compared with production costs. They do not tell us how much StreamVault should pay to license a film.

## Three franchise priorities

| Franchise | Films reviewed | Cinema earnings | Earnings compared with production costs |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Avengers | 4 | $7.92bn | 7.13x |
| Harry Potter | 8 | $7.76bn | 6.06x |
| Pirates of the Caribbean | 5 | $4.53bn | 3.62x |

These three franchises were also the strongest performers in the films released from 2000 onward.

## Five individual films to consider

These films earned the most among titles that were not recorded as part of a franchise in our data. This does not confirm that they are standalone films.

- **Inception** – $839.0m
- **Interstellar** – $746.6m
- **Forrest Gump** – $677.4m
- **WALL·E** – $521.3m
- **Schindler's List** – $321.4m

## Directors and actors worth noting

Peter Jackson's six films earned a combined $5.85bn. Steven Spielberg's eight films earned $4.45bn, while Christopher Nolan's seven earned $4.20bn.

Daniel Radcliffe, Rupert Grint and Emma Watson each appeared in eight of the films reviewed. Robert Downey Jr. appeared in seven.

These figures show past performance in our list. They do not guarantee future success.

## Deal priority: Avengers

I recommend making the Avengers franchise the first priority for further discussions. Its four films earned $7.92bn at cinemas against production costs of $1.11bn.

This makes it a strong candidate for negotiation, but it does not tell us what the rights should cost. Before making an offer, StreamVault should confirm which films are available, what rights are included, the studio's asking price and whether the titles are likely to attract viewers.

## What we still need to confirm

The brief refers to 127 films, but the file provided contains only 126 entries. We successfully retrieved information for 112 films. Five film IDs could not be found, and the original source lists were not available to help us check them.

We also could not confirm:

- Whether each franchise includes all the films available for licensing.
- Whether the films' reported earnings and production costs are accurate.
- Which films and rights the studios are willing to offer.
- The prices studios will charge.
- How much demand these films will attract on StreamVault.

Older film earnings have also not been adjusted for inflation.

## Decision requested

Prioritise discussions with the rights holders for Avengers, Harry Potter and Pirates of the Caribbean. Keep the five individual films as alternatives. Confirm the available titles, rights and prices before committing the $14M budget.
